Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

SamStudio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Dice mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) directly from you. However, the application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

2. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services which have their own Privacy Policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used for the core functionality of the Android app.
  • AdMob: We show advertisements to keep the app free. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show personalized ads.
  • Google Play Billing: Used to process in-app purchases (Premium/Lifetime). All transaction data is handled securely by Google.

3. Log Data

We want to inform you that whenever you use our Service, in a case of an error in the app, we collect data and information (through third-party products) on your phone called Log Data. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

4. Hardware Features

The app accesses the Accelerometer sensor of your device to provide the "Shake to Roll" feature. This data is processed locally on your device and is never stored or transmitted to our servers.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
