Privacy Policy

Welcome to Volume Booster. Your privacy is important to us.

1. Information Collection

We do not collect any personal identification information from our users. The app requires Microphone access only for visualizer processing, and the audio data is processed locally on your device and never stored or transmitted.

2. Device Permissions

The app may request access to Audio Settings and Notification Access to provide volume control and media synchronization features.

3. Ads & Billing

We use Google AdMob for advertisements and Google Play Billing for premium subscriptions. These services may collect device identifiers to provide personalized ads and process payments securely.

4. Data Security

We implement local security measures to protect your settings and preferences stored on the device.

5. Changes

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes.
