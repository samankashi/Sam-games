Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Animal Vision is a simulation app built for educational and entertainment purposes. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, images, or videos captured through the application. All image processing for animal vision simulation is performed locally and in real-time on your device.

2. Permissions

To provide its core functionality, the app requires the following permissions:

  • Camera: Used solely to provide the live animal vision simulation. No images are transmitted to our servers.
  • Storage/Gallery: Used only when you explicitly choose to save a captured photo to your device's gallery.

3. Advertisements

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use anonymous data (such as device identifiers) to show personalized or non-personalized ads. You can find Google's privacy policy regarding ads here: Google Privacy & Terms.

4. In-App Purchases

Payments for the Premium version are handled securely by the Google Play Billing system. We do not have access to your credit card or financial information.
