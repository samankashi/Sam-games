Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Phone Finder collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information Collection

We collect only the data necessary for the app to function. Specifically:

  • Audio Data: This is processed locally on your device for sound detection (like clapping or whistling). We do NOT record, store, or upload your audio to any servers.
  • Camera Data: Used exclusively for the "Intruder Selfie" feature. Captured images are stored locally on your device and are never shared or uploaded.

2. Permissions

To provide its core features, the app requires the following permissions:

  • Microphone: To detect sounds when the service is active.
  • Camera: To take photos of potential intruders.
  • Notifications: To keep the service running in the background and alert you.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information, such as your device's advertising ID, to show relevant ads. You can review AdMob's privacy policy on Google's website.

4. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. Since most of your data stays on your device, you have primary control over its security.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
