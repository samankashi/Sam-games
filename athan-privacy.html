Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 06, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Athan Guide handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Data Collection

Athan Guide is designed to be a privacy-first application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, such as your name, email address, or phone number, to any external servers.

2. Local Storage

All your preferences, including your preferred prayer calculation methods, language settings, and theme choices, are stored locally on your device. This data remains on your phone and is deleted if you uninstall the application.

3. Location Data

The app requires access to your device's location to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction. This location data is processed on-device only and is never shared or stored by us.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use anonymous data (such as device identifiers) to show relevant ads. You can learn more about how Google manages data in their Privacy & Terms.
